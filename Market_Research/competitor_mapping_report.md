## Executive Summary

This report provides a competitive intelligence analysis of three prominent business consultancy and advisory firms in the Indian market: **Naavbharat**, **StartupFlora**, and **Enego**. The objective is to map their operational footprints, service portfolios, pricing strategies, and customer acquisition funnels to serve as a benchmark for **Growthora Advisory Private Limited**. 

Key findings indicate that while all three competitors target the MSME and startup ecosystems, their positioning differs significantly:
*   **StartupFlora** focuses heavily on end-to-end "handholding," international expansion, and digital services, emphasizing a highly structured team approach.
*   **Enego** positions itself as a large-scale growth advisory with over 150 employees, emphasizing 90-day growth engagements and funding consultancy. 
*   **Naavbharat** operates fundamentally as a strategic consulting and compliance partner, heavily focused on bridging the gap between MSMEs and government schemes.

None of the competitors publicly disclose specific pricing packages, opting instead for a lead-generation model that drives prospects into customized 1-on-1 consultation calls.
