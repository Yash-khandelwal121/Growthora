# Growthora Finance & Funding Solutions

## IVP (TN)
A government initiative designed to encourage startups and MSMEs to utilize the services of knowledge providers to innovate and develop new products.

**What it is:** A grant up to ₹5 Lakhs (or 80% of project cost) offered by the Tamil Nadu government to help MSMEs fund innovation, R&D, and product development.
**Why Important:** It reduces the financial risk of innovation and allows MSMEs to partner with premier research institutions.
**Who Needs it:** Tamil Nadu based manufacturing or service MSMEs looking to develop a prototype or new commercial product.

**What you get:**
- Eligibility assessment for IVP
- Preparation of project proposal
- Identification of Knowledge Partner (KP)
- Application filing and documentation
- Assistance with queries during evaluation
- Guidance on fund utilization and reporting

## Global AI Innovation Challenge
A premier international grant and incubator program aimed at early-stage AI startups building scalable, transformative technologies.

**What it is:** An equity-free grant and mentorship program offered by global tech consortiums to support the development of ethical, high-impact AI.
**Why Important:** Provides crucial early-stage capital and access to top-tier cloud credits and AI compute resources.
**Who Needs it:** AI-first startups, researchers commercializing AI tech, and deep-tech founders.

**What you get:**
- Pitch deck and proposal refinement
- Technical architecture documentation support
- Application strategy and positioning
- Video pitch preparation
- Interview/Jury presentation coaching

## Growth Grant
Identify and apply for sector-agnostic growth grants aimed at helping established small businesses scale their operations, hire talent, and expand geographically.

**What it is:** Grants provided by government bodies or large corporate CSR wings to support business expansion and job creation.
**Why Important:** Allows companies to scale operations without taking on high-interest debt or diluting equity.
**Who Needs it:** Post-revenue startups and MSMEs with a proven business model ready to scale.

**What you get:**
- Identification of active Growth Grants
- Business plan refinement for scale
- Financial projections modeling
- Application and documentation packaging
- Post-grant utilization advisory

## Spark Grant
Designed for ideation and early-prototype stage founders, the Spark grant helps cover initial expenses like market research, MVP development, and patent filing.

**What it is:** A micro-grant (typically offered by incubators or state startup policies) to convert an innovative idea into a tangible prototype.
**Why Important:** It bridges the critical 'valley of death' funding gap before a startup is ready for angel investment.
**Who Needs it:** Student entrepreneurs, solo founders, and teams at the ideation stage.

**What you get:**
- Incubator matching and application
- Pitch deck creation for ideation stage
- Market sizing and research validation
- Mentorship on MVP development roadmap
- Application for state startup seed funds

## Grant for Textile Industry
Leverage central and state government schemes designed specifically to boost the textile sector, upgrade machinery, and promote exports.

**What it is:** Capital subsidies and grants under schemes like ATUFS (Amended Technology Upgradation Fund Scheme) and PM MITRA to support the textile value chain.
**Why Important:** Helps offset the high capital expenditure required for modernizing looms, processing units, and garmenting machinery.
**Who Needs it:** Textile manufacturers, weavers, garment exporters, and processing units.

**What you get:**
- Scheme eligibility assessment (ATUFS, State Policies)
- DPR (Detailed Project Report) preparation
- Machinery specification and quotation review
- UID generation and application filing on iTUFS portal
- Liaisoning with banks for term loan approval
- JIT (Joint Inspection Team) audit support
