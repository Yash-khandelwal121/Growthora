## Overview
Growthora Advisory Private Limited is a premier business consulting and advisory firm based in India. We help businesses, MSMEs, startups, and enterprises scale, stay compliant, and secure funding.

## Our Core Focus
We provide end-to-end support across:
- **Registration**: Company incorporation, MSME Udyam, FSSAI, ISO, Udyam, etc.
- **Finance & Funding**: Grants, Debt, Equity, Startup India Seed Fund, Government Schemes, Business Loans.
- **Certifications**: Quality credentials like ISO, ZED, Startup India.
- **Branding & Operations**: Market positioning and backend operational setup.
- **Legal & CA**: Audits, IP (Trademarks/Patents), Compliance.
- **MSME Benefits**: Capital grants, loan subsidies, and state incentives.
- **Valuation & IPO**: Independent valuations, financial models, and end-to-end IPO support.
