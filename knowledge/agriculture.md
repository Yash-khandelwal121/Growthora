## Overview
Agriculture, forestry, and fishing are critical to India's economy. At Growthora, we work with FPOs, agri-tech ventures, and processing units. We pair RKVY-RAFTAAR-style grants with term loans for machinery, storage, and irrigation.

## Typical Registrations Needed
1. **Udyam / MSME registration**: For government subsidies and lower interest rates.
2. **FSSAI license**: Mandatory for agri-processing and food distribution.
3. **Agmark / GI certification**: Quality certification for specialized produce.
4. **FPO registration**: Legal structure for farmer collectives.
5. **Soil health & organic certification**: NPOP and PGS-India certifications.

## Key Pain Points We Solve
- **Fragmented land holdings**: Overcoming the lack of immovable collateral for term loans.
- **Post-harvest losses**: Funding for pack-houses, reefer trucks, and solar cold stores.
- **Seasonal cash flow**: Restructuring EMI structures to match crop harvest cycles.
